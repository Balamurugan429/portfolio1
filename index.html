3. Flagship Research Project

Explainable, Extraction-Aware Ensemble Learning for Groundwater Level Prediction and Criticality Classification

Target Domain: Hydrogeological Depletion Forecasting & Aquifer Risk Management
Research Timeline: 2025 – 2026
Code Repository: github.com/Balamurugan429

Project Abstract & Scientific Methodology

Standard regression models often struggle when predicting dynamic groundwater tables due to non-linear seasonal recharge, localized extraction patterns, and varying rainfall distributions. To solve this, this research implements a hybrid ensemble framework combining three complementary tree-based algorithms with post-hoc SHAP and LIME interpretability:

  • Multi-Model Regressor Stacking: Combines XGBoost (45% weighting), LightGBM (35% weighting), and Random Forest (20% weighting) to minimize variance and eliminate extreme outliers.
  • Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution: Employs SHAP TreeExplainer and local LIME surrogates to compute exact Shapley feature contributions for each localized well forecast.
  • Extraction-Rate Weighting: Incorporates aquifer drawdowns, historical water-table lag vectors, and rainfall infiltration coefficients.
  • Criticality Categorization: Automatically maps predicted water levels into standardized risk zones (Safe, Semi-Critical, Critical, Over-Exploited).
